# Phase 1 data inventory

| Data                                        | Purpose                                         | Classification | Retention decision                                       |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Auth email and credential metadata          | Account identity and recovery                   | Confidential   | Account term plus legally approved closure period        |
| Vet name and phone                          | Professional onboarding and contact             | Confidential   | Account term plus approved business/legal retention      |
| Licence number and verification status      | Restrict clinical use to eligible professionals | Confidential   | Account term and verification/audit period               |
| Device identifier and activity times        | Session security and revocation                 | Confidential   | Active device term plus security investigation window    |
| Audit event identifiers and action metadata | Security, accountability, dispute evidence      | Restricted     | Retention to be approved with Ghana counsel/DPC guidance |

No client, patient, clinical, diagnostic, payment, or AI data is introduced in Phase 1.
